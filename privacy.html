Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 July 2026

1. What this covers

This policy explains what information PRIMETEK collects through www.primetek.online — including our contact form, shop, cart and checkout — and how it's used.

2. Information we collect

  • Contact & order details: name, email, phone number, business name, and any project or order notes you submit through our forms.
  • Cart contents: the products and quantities you add to your cart are stored only in your browser's local storage on your device — we do not see this until you submit a checkout order.
  • Communications: messages you send us via email or WhatsApp, and our replies.

We do not collect payment card details through this site, as payments are arranged directly with our team after an order is placed.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to enquiries and process orders.
  • To deliver purchased products and provide related support.
  • To send order or project updates relevant to work you've requested.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for unrelated marketing without your consent.

4. Third-party services

Form submissions on this site are processed by FormSubmit, a third-party form-handling service, which delivers your message to our inbox. Order enquiries may also be sent via WhatsApp if you choose that option. These services handle data according to their own privacy policies.

5. Cookies & local storage

We use your browser's local storage to remember cart contents between pages on this site. This data stays on your device and is not a tracking cookie. We do not currently use analytics or advertising cookies.

6. Data retention

We retain enquiry and order information for as long as needed to fulfil orders, maintain business records, and meet legal/accounting obligations (including GST record-keeping).

7. Your rights

You can ask us to review, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us using the details below.

8. Contact

For any privacy questions, contact info@primetek.online or 080 6218 1385.
