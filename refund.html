Refund Policy

Last updated: 12 July 2026

1. Before work begins

If you've paid but we haven't yet started work on your order (no design, build, or account setup has begun), you can cancel for a full refund by contacting us within 48 hours of payment.

2. Once work has started

Because our ready-made products are customized with your branding, content and (for apps) store setup as soon as work begins, refunds after work has started are handled as follows:

  • Cancelling after work has started but before delivery: refund of the amount paid, minus a proportionate charge for work already completed.
  • Once a product has been fully delivered (files handed over / app submitted to a store on your behalf), it is considered fulfilled and is not eligible for a refund, except as described in Section 3.

3. Faulty or not-as-described products

If a delivered product is materially broken or doesn't match what was listed at purchase, contact us within 7 days of delivery. We will first attempt to fix the issue at no extra cost; if we cannot resolve it within a reasonable time, we'll offer a partial or full refund depending on the extent of the issue.

4. What isn't covered

  • Change of mind after a product has been delivered and accepted.
  • Costs of third-party services you purchase separately (domain names, app store developer fees, hosting from other providers).
  • Delays caused by late or missing content/approvals from your side.

5. Custom projects

Custom-scoped projects (as described under Pricing on our homepage) follow the refund/cancellation terms of their specific written agreement, which takes precedence over this general policy.

6. How to request a refund

Email info@primetek.online or WhatsApp 080 6218 1385 with your order details. Approved refunds are processed back to the original payment method within 7–10 business days.
